# MY FINDINGS
- Interesting and simple paper on **gray failures** and issues they cause , **2017 published** quite old but has a interesting take on how to look at failures of system in cloud systems , and how to combat them . 
- Mainly **Gray failures** are situations in cloud systems when we cannot detect failure and our systems behave weirdly , Availability is major concern when you cannot detect something is wrong and act accordingly . Mainly because they consider a overlay model ie check if a component is correct or failure .
- The propose a solution based on bridging the gap between components 's perceptive of what it means to fail  is called differential observbility.
- Mask these gray faliures ( covering up the mistake) , will eventually lead to system with too many failed components , so we need to approach this head on , Byzantine fault tolerant state machine is one such protocol .
- High redundancy hurts , an example of multiple routers and host clients , the statement is having more redundant routers looks like increasing availability but the truth is that it increase the odds of request involving all routers , meaning it could passed through in router at the end , so if grey failure occurs in any one router , it will effect severly on every request and system as a whole .
- Storage server which was botched because it could not handle the write volume , even after reboot will still face the same problem , and remember that because the system could not detect is error properly , it will inevitiable slow down the system . When we finally get a report and diagnose it , we remove the failing sever entirely , now the entire load is then put on healthy nodes/servers , a possible cascading failure could take place .
- Wrong diagnosing of errors by the fail detector software can create another layer of burden to navigate and fix .... blame game between corporate teams here .
- Coming to a simple solution was to use a system (observer + reactor) which search s for faults on the apps ( servers/applications ..etc) , and also the app can inform the system of any possible issues (io time exceeding seen patterns etc) , and we always move from a 
		Possible gray failure (tiny amount of solwness) -> Complete failure -> Purging
- We can solve the few examples using this model we made here , so differential observerbility plays a major role .
- We more ideas to further try to make this model better are , Use multiple metrics to diagnose over just one metric like heartbeats , A complete distributed Plane of observbility which includes information of all components not just the core , Approximating issues using techniques like Probing messages in application where tracking clients 's environment is not useful  , A way to analyse temporal patterns of gray failures ie cascading failures (AI comes to mind here ...) .
- Conclusion is about how current solutions treat this problem as a fail-stop problem (either works or failed) , but its not that way as we have working nodes with issues (gray failure) .
